Four ways to work,
one memory behind them.

A mode changes how it answers, never what it knows. Pick one on Telegram and you have picked it on Discord and here — it is stored as a memory like everything else.

Tutor · teaches, and remembers where you got stuck

Hand it your notes and let it ask you about them

Works in steps, checks each one has landed, and starts where you stopped last time.

  • Send a PDF or your notes and it reads them — scanned pages it will tell you it cannot read.
  • It writes questions from the document's own words, four options, one tap each.
  • Getting one wrong is stored like any other memory, so it can bring it back on another app days later.

One runnable fix rather than a survey of approaches, and it never asks you to repeat your setup.

Planner · deals in dates

Pins the vague thing to a real day

Finishes with the single next thing to do, not a list of options.
